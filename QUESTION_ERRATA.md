# Question wording notes

The source questions and answer mappings remain unchanged. This file records wording that may conflict with, or oversimplify, current authoritative guidance.

## Broadcast impartiality and “equal time”

- Question IDs: `exam-01-q19`, `exam-04-q24`, `exam-11-q10`
- Source wording: the questions state that television and radio must give rival political viewpoints “equal time”.
- Exam answer retained: television and radio; the true/false item remains `True`.
- Current qualification: Sections 319 and 320 of the Communications Act 2003 require statutory broadcast standards for due impartiality. Ofcom states that due impartiality does not mean equal airtime for every view. During an election, parties and independent candidates must receive due weight, taking account of evidence such as past and current electoral support, while significant perspectives must also be considered.
- Decision: retain the authorised question wording and scoring, but show the qualification in the post-answer explanation.
- Sources: Ofcom Broadcasting Code Sections Five and Six; Communications Act 2003.

## Channel Islands classified as an overseas territory

- Question ID: `exam-01-q20`
- Source explanation: “The Channel Islands is a British overseas territory linked to the UK, but it is not part of it.”
- Exam answer retained: `The Channel Islands` is a Crown dependency and is not part of the UK.
- Correction: the Channel Islands are not a British Overseas Territory. They comprise the Bailiwick of Jersey and the Bailiwick of Guernsey, both self-governing Crown Dependencies.
- Decision: retain the authorised question and answer mapping, but replace the inaccurate post-answer explanation.
- Sources: Government of Jersey; UK Government Crown Dependencies fact sheet.
